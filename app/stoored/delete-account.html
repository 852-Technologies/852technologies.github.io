Delete your Stoored account and data

Stoored Privacy Policy

Stoored, by 852 Technologies, keeps your inventory and your optional login on your phone only. There is no online Stoored account, so everything can be deleted on the phone itself. Only records of Stoored Pro purchases are held outside your phone, by our purchase-management provider, and you can ask us to delete those.

Your login account

The optional login (username, password, authenticator app and recovery email) exists only on your phone. To delete it: open Stoored, go to More > Security, switch off Enable Login and confirm with your password. The account is deleted from your phone straight away. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Your inventory data

Items, purchases and usage records, photos, categories, locations, people and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them: More > Import / Export > Wipe Data, or uninstall the app. Backup and export files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Purchase records

Stoored Pro purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, keeps a record of them linked to a random ID created for your installation of the app (not your name or email address). To have these records deleted, email sales@852technologies.com with the Support ID shown in the app under About > Application Info. We will delete them within 30 days and confirm by email. Your store account keeps its own record of what you bought, so you can still restore purchases afterwards.

Emails you send us

If you contact us, we keep your message only as long as needed to reply and as described in the Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete it at the same address.
