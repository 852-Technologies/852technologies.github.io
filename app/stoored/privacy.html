Stoored Pro, purchases and free trial

Some features (“Stoored Pro features”) require a purchase. Each is a one-time purchase of a licence to use that feature in the App; it is not a subscription and does not renew. “Unlock All” is a one-time purchase covering every Stoored Pro feature, including Stoored Pro features we add later. Prices are shown in the App and by the app store before you buy, in your local currency, and may include tax.

Purchases are made and paid for through Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms, using the store account you are signed in to. We do not receive or store your payment details. Refunds, if any, are handled by the store under its refund policy and applicable law. To restore purchases on a new or reset device, use Restore Purchases in the App while signed in to the same store account.

For a limited period after you first install the App (currently 14 days), all Stoored Pro features are available free of charge as a trial. When the trial ends, Stoored Pro features you have not purchased are no longer available; data you created with them stays on your device. We may offer introductory or limited-time prices, shown in the App together with the period they apply to. We may change prices for future purchases; a change does not affect a purchase already made.

A purchased feature remains available for as long as we offer and support the App on your platform. If we have to remove or materially change a purchased feature, we will act reasonably and provide any remedy required by applicable law or the store’s rules.

Privacy and device data

The current version of Stoored has no 852 Technologies server and no advertising or analytics service. Your inventory, photos, settings and optional device-local login profile are stored on your device, not in an online account with us. Some data is processed by third-party services only as described below.

When you request a barcode lookup, the barcode number is sent to the third-party lookup services described above. Like other internet requests, those services also receive technical request data such as your IP address and may process it under their own privacy policies. No other inventory fields are intentionally included in the lookup request.

To sell and restore Stoored Pro features, the App uses Google Play or Apple App Store billing and RevenueCat, a purchase-management service provided by RevenueCat, Inc. The App sends RevenueCat a random identifier created for this installation of the App (not your name or email address), your purchase and restore records, and technical data such as the app version, platform, store country and IP address. RevenueCat and the store process this data under their own privacy policies to verify purchases and tell the App which features you own. Your inventory data is not sent.

The App accesses the camera or photo library only when you choose a feature that needs it, such as scanning a barcode or adding a photo, and subject to your device permissions. Photos remain device-local unless you choose to export, share or back them up.

When you create or share a backup, you choose the destination. The recipient or storage provider you select—including a cloud-storage, file or email provider—may receive the backup and related technical data under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you choose to send a problem report, your email app opens with the report so that you can review it before sending. It contains technical information—the app version, device model and operating system, app language, this installation’s support ID, app settings, counts of stored records, recently opened screens and actions, and recent error details—but not item names, photos or login details.

If you contact us, we receive the contact details and message content you provide. We may use and retain them as reasonably necessary to respond, provide support, protect legal rights, prevent abuse and comply with law. We do not sell personal information.

Because we do not possess your device-local App data, you normally access, correct or delete it using the App or your device, including by clearing App data or uninstalling. Purchase records held by RevenueCat can be deleted on request; please include the support ID shown in About > Application Info. Applicable law may give you additional rights. For privacy questions or requests concerning information we may hold, contact sales@852technologies.com

Website feedback form

The Stoored website has an optional feedback form hosted by Tally (tally.so). If you submit it, Tally receives your answers (whether you like Stoored, which phone you use, and any feature ideas you type) and may receive technical data such as your IP address, and processes it under its own privacy policy. The form also has an optional name and email field for people who want to be told when an iPhone version is available; you can leave it blank and still submit your answers. If you fill it in, we use your name and email only to send you that notice, and we keep them until we have sent it or you ask us to delete them, which you can do by writing to sales@852technologies.com. Please do not put other personal information in your answers. We use the answers themselves only to decide what to improve or build next. The form is on the website only and is not part of the App.
