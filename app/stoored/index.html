Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. Stoored is designed to work without sign-up, so you can begin organizing immediately.

Where is my inventory stored?+

Your inventory is stored locally on your device. Stoored does not require a cloud account or backend sync.

Can Stoored help me reduce waste?+

Yes. Expiry tracking, waste insights, consumption history, and reorder guidance help you use more of what you already have.

Can I back up or move my data?+

Yes. Stoored supports encrypted backup and restore, along with CSV import and export tools.
